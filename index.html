How it works · Portaldot's own modules

The stop lives in the asset.

No contract in the enforcement path: the asset's roles sit on three multisigs, and the runtime does the refusing.

1-of-2watchers needed to freeze · M_watch

The breaker

A 1-of-2 watcher multisig freezes the asset once a watcher sees reserves fall below supply (12.22 s from drain to freeze in the V3 drill); the reason rides in the same extrinsic.

Multisig.as_multi_threshold_1Assets.freeze_asset

The inert freezer

A watcher cannot thaw what its freezer role froze: W1's thaw_asset through M_watch, the multisig W2 froze through, comes back Assets.NoPermission (170115-1).

Reserve-gated mint

The issuer is a 2-of-2 {operator, reserve bot}; the bot co-signs only if finalized reserves cover supply plus the amount.

M_mint 2-of-2

Gated thaw

The admin is a 2-of-3 {A, A2, B}; the bot co-signs a thaw only after finality has passed the drain block (170133-1). It refused one earlier: finalized anvil block #1559 was older than the drain at anvil block #1622. A and A2 together could still thaw without the bot.

M_admin 2-of-3

Below the waterline

What it does not do

In the README's own words. A judge should know where the line is before trusting it. All of them are in the README.

Custody is trusted: the vault owner can move reserves. Ballast makes that visible and stops the shortfall from spreading.

Questions a judge would ask

Is this really on Portaldot V3?

Yes: 79 V3 extrinsics on 2026-10-24, each linked to node-console in docs/PROOF.md and re-queried as final, again on 2026-10-25. Genesis, :code hash and spec portaldot-v3/3002 are pinned by the day-0 check (evidence/v3-day0-20261020T152616Z.json).

Is the reserve on Sepolia?

No. In the V3 runs the reserve is a local anvil chain; the Sepolia vault is not deployed yet (it needs 100.00 test USDC). Every receipt names its chain.

Is this a trustless bridge?

No. Custody is trusted; Ballast makes a drain visible and stops the shortfall from being minted over or spreading.

Can a watcher unfreeze the asset?

No. W1's thaw_asset through M_watch, the multisig W2 froze through, returned Assets.NoPermission (170115-1). A thaw needs two of the three M_admin keys (A, A2, B). The bot co-signs only after finality passes the drain (170133-1), but A and A2 together could thaw without it.

Who holds the keys?

In this build one person holds every operator key; the separation is enforced by the runtime, not by independent people. Root can override every role (V3 has a Sudo key set; who controls it is not known to us).

What happens if the bot stops?

Nothing is enforced while it is stopped; the heartbeat rule freezes the asset if a watcher is running, and ReserveOracle.isBacked(maxAge) reads false when stale. That is the design; the outage and heartbeat drills ran on the stand-in, not yet on V3.

The receipts are public. Check the line yourself.
