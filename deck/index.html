Ballast

A reserve-gated circuit breaker for a custodial test dollar on Portaldot.

The reserve line sinks below the load line — the breaker snaps shut, and pUSDC freezes on Portaldot.

On the Portaldot V3 testnet, 2026-10-24: watcher W2 froze asset 4401 in extrinsic 170112-1. Week 2 check-in · v0.0.1

Ballast is a circuit breaker for a custodial test dollar. When the vault's reserves fall below what has been minted, a watcher freezes the asset on Portaldot. This drawing is a real moment: block 170,112 on the V3 testnet, on October 24.
The problem

Reserves drain. Nobody sees.

As far as we found, Portaldot has no stable unit of account. The realistic first step is a custodial wrapped dollar, and when its reserves drain, every holder keeps trading an IOU that is already worth less than its face value.

A wrapped dollar is custodial: someone holds the real dollars. If they drain the vault, nothing on the token side notices. Holders keep paying each other in a token that is no longer fully backed. That gap between steps two and three is what Ballast closes.
What it does

Four roles, three multisigs

RoleHeld byWhat it may do
FreezerM_watch 1-of-2watchers W1, W2Any one watcher freezes the asset when reserves < supply. Its thaw attempts fail with Assets.NoPermission.
IssuerM_mint 2-of-2operator A, reserve bot BThe bot co-signs a mint only if finalized reserves cover supply plus the amount, and never while frozen.
AdminM_admin 2-of-3A, A2, BThe bot co-signs a thaw only after finality passes the drain block and reserves cover supply again.
Recordbot Bremarks + EVM mirrorBALLAST/1 attestations on chain, mirrored into ReserveOracle; ballast verify re-reads both chains.

Roles are set with Assets.set_team; the reserve check itself runs off chain, in the bot and the watchers.

Three multisigs hold the asset's three roles. Watchers can only freeze. Minting needs the reserve bot's signature, and the bot checks finalized reserves first. Thawing needs the bot too, and only after finality has passed the drain. The bot also writes a public record.

The Portaldot runtime enforces the asset's roles and the multisig thresholds; remarks and a read-only oracle on Revive carry the record. The reserve checks run in off-chain processes that each hold one key. The reserve vault sits on an EVM chain; in these drills that is a local anvil chain, with Sepolia as the target.
Why it's new

The stop lives in the asset

QuestionA reserve number on its ownBallast, measured on V3
Who stops transfers?each dApp that chose to read itthe runtime refuses Assets.transfer → AssetNotLive (170114-1; other transfer paths not drilled)
Can the stopper restart it?the number carries no role on the assetno: a watcher's thaw → NoPermission (170115-1)
When can it restart?whenever a consumer decidesthe bot co-signs a thaw only once finalized reserves pass the drain: refused at anvil #1559 < #1622, co-signed in 170133-1. A and A2 together could still thaw without it.
Can someone fake the record?depends on who signs the feeda forged remark from another key (170253-1) → ballast verify REJECTED; 4/4 bot attestations PASS

Built from pallet-native modules (Assets roles, Multisig, System remarks) with no contract code in the enforcement path. The one contract, ReserveOracle, is a read-only mirror: it cannot freeze or thaw anything.

A reserve number alone tells you something is wrong and leaves each app to act on it. Ballast puts the stop in the asset's own roles, so the runtime refuses the frozen asset's transfers, the freezer cannot undo it, and the bot will not co-sign a thaw before finality passes the drain. The record is checkable, and a forged one is rejected.

Users and model

Who needs a load line

The first issuer of a dollar on Portaldot, who needs a safety layer it can show, and that the ecosystem can require of any issuer.

The dApp builder deciding whether checkout may accept that dollar, who must tell users why it is safe.

Funding (plan): an ecosystem grant to reach a Sepolia-backed deployment with outside watchers; after that, the dollar's issuer runs Ballast as the safety layer the ecosystem asks of it. Today: no external users and no revenue.

The user is whoever issues the first dollar on Portaldot, and the builders who would accept it. Today this is a testnet build with one operator. The next steps are a real Sepolia reserve and watchers run by other people, funded by a grant. We have no users or revenue yet, and we say so.
