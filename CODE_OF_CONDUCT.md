# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment: using welcoming and inclusive language, being respectful of differing viewpoints, gracefully accepting constructive criticism, and focusing on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and publishing others' private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying these standards and may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct. Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainers through a private GitHub security advisory or a direct message to the repository owner.
